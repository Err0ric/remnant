| D231 | Vendor alert cap and messages per source (owner, 2026-11-09, ADR-0049 0b). Cap: a source may create 100 new alert items in any rolling hour; past it the source pauses. High and Critical alerts still raise during a pause (they skip the cap); Medium and below are held: stored and searchable, not raised, and a held alert resent as High or Critical raises. The pause ends on its own once the last hour is back under 100. One notice per pause, sent when it ends, says how long it lasted and how many alerts were held by severity, to the source's chosen targets; Health and the Sources page show the pause and the held counts while it lasts; pause and resume are audited `source.vendor_alerts_paused` and `source.vendor_alerts_resumed`. Notify: a per-source "Notify alert targets" switch, off by default, with the source's own targets and a minimum severity (default High); when on, a new vendor alert item or a severity rise is sent once, after the cap, never for a held alert or a muted item. Changes are admin-only and audited `source.vendor_alert_change` | A noisy or forged source must not bury real alerts (T81, T82) or flood alert targets; vendors usually send their own messages, so ours start off | 2026-11-09 |
