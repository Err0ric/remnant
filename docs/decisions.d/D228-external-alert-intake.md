| D228 | External alert intake (ADR-0049 build item 0a): a template with `emits: alerts` and `alert: {id, product}` maps to Detection Finding (2004) and is active only once an admin approves it (ADR-0005). A complete alert keeps its key under `unmapped._alert`; a short one is stored `partial` with `alert_missing:<fields>` and raises nothing. Each minute a built-in check reads alerts stored since its last look (event time within 7 days, through the guard) and raises one item per product and vendor alert ID: kind `rule`, subject `ext:<product>:<title>`, the vendor's severity as High or Medium (a low or informational alert gets no severity, since Alerts shows only those two, and its level is named in the item's reason), grouped per entity, title, and day (D200). The key is kept in `external_alert_keys`, so a resend raises nothing and a resend at a higher severity raises the open item's. The syslog header's host (the sensor) is never the subject unless the template maps it. Not in this item: the per-source cap (0b), linked items on a closed item (0c), and notifications to alert targets, which ADR-0049 does not specify (owner question) | Phase 0 first, so triage has alerts to work on; reusing the `rule` kind avoids rebuilding `alert_items` and keeps external alerts in the same class as Remnant's own detections | 2026-11-09 |
