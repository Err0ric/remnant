| D214 | Hooks plugin records not delivered: idle versus still sending (owner review item 9; narrows D166). A hooks plugin machine whose queue holds records and that delivered no hook record for `agents.queue_stuck_days` is told apart by whether it still sends any other telemetry (Claude Code OpenTelemetry, the MCP inventory, or other events from that machine name) after its last hook record and in the last 24 hours. Still sending: unhealthy on Health and one medium alert item per machine and episode (as D166). Sending nothing at all: idle or offline, attention on Health with the queue depth and last contact, info, and no alert item, because the alert severities stay high and medium (ADR-0043) and no low level is added to the stored field. A plugin that does not report its queue and delivers no hook record while the machine still sends other telemetry is a medium item that says the depth is not reported. The item's detail gives the records waiting (or "not reported by the plugin") and the last contact, by hooks and by other telemetry; its time range starts at the last hook record | Proposed, pending review |
