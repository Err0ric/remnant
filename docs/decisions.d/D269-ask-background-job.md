A failed job is a 200 with its error, never a 5xx. The job keeps the asker's identity from the submit; grants are read as each step starts, as for any query; if the session ends the job still finishes and stays readable only by that user. Nobody else reads a job, admins included (404), as with Ask answers today. One job per person at a time (the existing one-Ask rule), at most 16 running in the process, model calls waiting in the existing gate queue (ADR-0028); the person's turn comes back before the job shows finished; jobs are kept in memory for 30 minutes after they finish, at most 200, and none survives a restart. No cancellation. The synchronous calls stay for the current page until it moves to jobs.
